A pet for your desktop, in short.

Roamies is a small pixel pet that lives on top of your screen. It walks your taskbar, sits on your windows, gets you to stretch, and visits your friends. It's free, for Windows 10 and 11.

How we do things

  • FreeNothing in Roamies costs real money. Coins come from using it: a coin a minute, stretch breaks, quests, visits.
  • PrivateNo ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your save is one file on your own computer. The privacy page lists everything else, in plain words.
  • FairThe capsule machine has its odds printed on it, and it makes up for a run of bad luck.
  • SafeBetween players, every word comes from a list; a pet's name is the only text of your own anyone sees. Meeting other players is off until you turn it on.
  • PoliteIt gets out of the way of games and anything full screen, and doesn't ask for a stretch during a call.
  • HomeA pet that goes visiting always comes back, after 100 seconds at the latest.
  • HeardThe speech bubble in the app reaches the people who make Roamies. We read the notes, and may write back.
  • OpenEvery version's changes are written down, and when something breaks the status page says so.

Questions

Does it get in the way of games?

No. Over a full-screen game it moves to another monitor, or hides until you're done. Easy mode in Settings lightens it on a modest computer, and such a computer turns it on by itself.

Windows won't run the installer

The installer isn't code-signed, so some PCs block a new version of it. Right-click Roamies-Setup.exe, choose Properties, tick Unblock, press OK, then run it. Or get Roamies from the Microsoft Store.

Do I need an account?

No. Your copy makes up a friend code by itself the first time it runs. Signing in with Discord is optional: it lets friends add you by your Discord name.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The pet, the wardrobe, capsules, quests and the arcade all live on your computer. Only friends, visits and other players in the town need the friend server. The status page says how it's doing.

Is there a Mac version?

Planned, not tested yet. Today Roamies is built and tested on Windows 10 and 11.

Something's broken, or I have an idea

Tell us from inside the app: the speech bubble next to the sound button. You get a reference, and the reply shows up next to your note. Outside the app, message @roamiesapp on X or open an issue on GitHub.

How do I remove it?

Uninstall it like any app. The installer's copy leaves your save in %APPDATA%\roamies, so installing again brings the same pet back; delete that folder to remove it too. The privacy page covers what the friend server holds.
