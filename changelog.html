What's new · latest v1.6.0

Every version, in a few lines.

Newest first. In the app, a new version arrives as a crate on a parachute, and your pet reads you the list. The long notes for each one are on GitHub.

  1. v1.6.014 Nov 2026

    The town

    • NewThe park is now a town in 3D: a street of lamps, an arcade, a playground, a pond and a hill, with weather and a real sky.
    • NewMeet other players there if you turn it on in Settings, or pin a note to the bulletin board.
    • ChangedA calmer app window with its own title bar, tidier Settings, and far less work while it sits open.
    • FixedA pet that couldn't be picked up after the screens went dark, and a pet lost on a visit.
  2. v1.5.114 Nov 2026

    A quick fix

    • FixedYour pet could stop letting you grab, pet or toss it until you restarted Roamies.
  3. v1.5.021 Oct 2026

    Out of the way, and a new server

    • ChangedThe pet keeps clear of anything full screen, videos and slides too, not only games. A setting keeps it out over videos if you'd rather.
    • ChangedThe friend server has a new home, so friends, visits and sign-in stay up all month.
  4. v1.4.014 Oct 2026

    The park

    • NewA park to steer your pet around, with your friends in it. Race to the flag, or play tag.
    • NewThe Play tab shows the top score of everyone in each arcade game.
    • NewA pet can have more than one sweetheart, in secret, with rumours and confessions.
    • ChangedA tidier Play tab, with the arcade in view without scrolling.
  5. v1.3.014 Oct 2026

    Stretch breaks

    • NewHalf a minute of stretching with your pet, worth 40 coins. It suggests one after an hour at the computer.
    • ChangedThe video break is gone: for many of you it never went off.
    • NewSigning in with Discord is worth 200 coins, once.
  6. v1.2.013 Oct 2026

    The Store, and packs

    • NewA Store tab for what you don't own yet. The Wardrobe is what's yours.
    • NewPacks fill as you claim your daily reward and quests, and you pick each prize.
    • NewGold, Diamond and Dark matter: three colors to work up to.
    • ChangedThe capsule machine makes up for bad luck, and easy mode lightens things on a modest computer.
  7. v1.1.012 Oct 2026

    Hangouts

    • NewStart a hangout and your friends' pets come over to your desktop. Gather calls the whole gang together.
  8. v1.0.012 Oct 2026

    One point oh

    • NewThe version that went to the Microsoft Store.
    • NewA speech bubble in the app sends us a bug, an idea or a kind word.
  9. v0.1.512 Oct 2026

    Looking after it

    • NewFood, Fun and Love meters, and mischief when a pet is left wanting.
    • NewSky Hop, a second arcade game, and 42 more things to wear.
    • NewFriendship levels and sweethearts.
    • NewUpdates arrive as a crate on a parachute.
  10. v0.111 Oct 2026

    The first one

    • NewA pet that roams your desktop, a wardrobe, a capsule machine, and visits to friends.
