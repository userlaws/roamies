The fine print, in plain words.

Roamies is a pet that lives on your desktop. Most of what it knows stays on your computer. This page says what doesn't, where it goes, and how to have it removed.

stays on your computer

Never sent anywhere

  • Your save. Coins, wardrobe, streaks, quests, friends list and settings are one file on your computer.
  • Your open windows. The pet reads their titles and positions so it can walk on them, have its say about the app in front, and keep out of the way of a game. It reads the title bar, not what is inside the window.
  • How long you've been at the computer. For stretch breaks the pet counts the time while the keyboard or mouse has been touched in the last two minutes, or while something is playing.
  • What is playing. The name of the song, so the pet can nod along. Settings > React to music turns that off.

The app has no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

the friend server

What friends need, and no more

While Roamies runs it is connected to the Roamies friend server, so friends can see who is around and pets can visit. The server keeps:

  • your friend code, which your copy makes up by itself. It is not your name.
  • your pet's name and what it is wearing.
  • which version of Roamies you run and where it was installed from (this website or the Microsoft Store), and when your copy first and last connected.
  • the friend codes on your friends list, as your copy last said, so gifts and a changed code reach only real friends.
  • friend requests, accepts and unfriends on their way to someone who is offline, for up to 30 days.
  • gifts Roamies has sent your copy, and whether they arrived.
  • gifts between friends: coins or an item, which line from the list went with it, who to and when, and whether it arrived. That is how the daily limits hold.
  • if you said who told you about Roamies, their friend code next to yours, and whether the two of you have been sent the Carrier pigeon.
  • if you changed your friend code, the old code next to the new one, so friends who were away can find you.
  • a ban and its reason, if a code or an account has ever been shut out.

Window titles, song names and anything else about your screen never reach it. Coins and ordinary items aren't there either: those live in your save.

The server's own code does not store your IP address. (A report from the photo wall, below, keeps a scrambled form of where it came from, only so that one person counts once, and only for 30 days.) It runs at Cloudflare and keeps its database at Neon, in the United States, and the hosts handle each connection the way any host does.

the town

Other players, only if you say so

The Town tab is a place your pet walks around in. Settings > Meet other players in the town is off until you turn it on. Off, your town only ever has the friends in your hangout in it.

  • With the switch on, whoever else is playing can share your town, up to eight pets in one. Their copies see your pet's name, what it looks like, where it walks, and what it says from the list of phrases. Someone who clicks your pet is shown its name, with Add friend, Hide, Block and Report. Their copy is told your friend code so that it can reach yours and ask to be friends; the app does not show it. Friends in your hangout see your pet in the town either way.
  • Through a relay. The copies in a town talk to each other directly, always through a relay run by Cloudflare, so no copy learns another's internet address.
  • What the friend server is told. While your pet is in the town, your copy says so every few seconds, with whether the switch is on, which hangout you are in, the friend codes you have blocked, and how much its town connections carried. The server uses that to decide who shares a town and holds it in memory. What it stores is one monthly total of what all the towns carried.
  • The bulletin board. A notice is a phrase and a sticker, each from a fixed list, signed with your pet's name. The friend server keeps it with your friend code. It is up for a day, for whoever opens the board in any town, whether or not they have the switch on. It is deleted from the server a day or two after it comes down. Pinning another replaces it.
  • Reports. Reporting a pet in your town sends the friend server your friend code, the reported copy's friend code, which town the two of you were in, and a reason from a list of four. The people who make Roamies read it, and can shut a copy out of every town. A report is deleted 90 days after it has been dealt with.

The phrases, the emotes and the notices all come from lists, so pet names are the only words of your own that other players see.

photos and your profile

Public only when you post it

  • The photo booth makes pictures on your computer. They go nowhere unless you copy, save or post one.
  • The photo wall. A photo you post goes on a page of this website for anyone to see: your pet's name and what it was wearing, the backdrop, the pose and the line under it (all from lists), and a friend's pet and name if you took it with one. The friend server keeps that with your friend code (and the friend's, if they are in it), which never appears on the page. A friend can only be in a posted photo while they are online and friends with you. You, or the friend in it, can take a photo down from the photo booth; ask us and all of yours are removed. If your pet's name is held or you are banned, your photos show that or come off. Anyone can report a photo from the page, once each; three reports take it off the wall until we look at it, and a report keeps a scrambled form of where it came from for 30 days.
  • Postcards from visits stay in your save.
  • Your Discord status. While Discord is open on the same computer, Roamies tells it, on that computer, that you are playing Roamies, with your pet's name and what it is up to, and Discord shows that on your profile. Never your friend code. Settings > Show my pet on Discord turns it off.
  • Your pet's birthday is in your save. Friends' copies are told the day, so they can show it.
signing in with Discord

Only if you choose to

Signing in is optional. It lets friends add you by your Discord name instead of your code.

  • Roamies asks Discord one thing: who you are. It receives your Discord id, username, display name and avatar. It does not get your email, your servers or your messages.
  • The friend server keeps those next to your friend code, and a sign-in token that is stored only in scrambled form. Discord's own token is used once and thrown away.
  • Your Discord name shows on your pet card and next to your pet in your friends' lists.
  • Signing out in Roamies removes the link between your Discord account and your friend code.
feedback

Only when you press Send

The speech bubble in the app is for telling us about a bug, an idea, or something you like. Nothing goes anywhere until you press Send. Then the friend server keeps:

  • what you wrote, and which kind of note you said it was.
  • your friend code and your pet's name, and your Discord account if you are signed in, so a note can be told from the next one and answered.
  • a few facts about your copy, so a bug can be tracked down: the version of Roamies and where it was installed from, your system (like Windows 11), how many screens you have and their scaling, whether the friend server was connected, how many friends are on your list, which pet you have and its size and mischief setting, and which tab was open.

Window titles, song names and anything else about your screen are not part of it. We read the notes, and may write a short reply that shows up next to yours in the app. Notes are kept so we can keep track of what was asked for and what was fixed; ask and yours are deleted.

updates, and this website

Other places Roamies talks to

  • Updates. A copy from the installer looks for new versions on GitHub and downloads them from there. A copy from the Microsoft Store is updated by the Store. So that Settings can say when a newer version is out, that copy also asks GitHub for the newest version's number, when it starts and then twice a day. The request carries nothing about you or your copy beyond what any web request carries. GitHub and Microsoft see those requests as they see any download.
  • This website. The demo pet's coins and outfit are kept in your own browser and sent nowhere. The page asks GitHub what the newest version is. It sets no cookies and has no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, and its fonts come from Google Fonts.
  • The status page. To say whether the friend server is up, the status page opens a connection to it from your browser and closes it again without sending anything. It also asks GitHub for the newest version and for our log of earlier checks. None of that carries anything about you beyond what any web request carries.
never

What Roamies does not do

  • It does not sell or hand on anything about you.
  • It does not show ads, in the app or here.
  • It does not read your files, or what is inside your windows.
  • It takes no payment: coins are earned by using the app, and there is nothing to buy with money.
your say

Asking, and having things removed

  • To have what the friend server holds about your friend code or your Discord account removed, or to ask anything about this page, message @roamiesapp on X or open an issue at github.com/userlaws/roamies. Your friend code is on the Friends tab; include it.
  • To remove everything on your computer, uninstall Roamies. The installer's copy leaves your save behind in %APPDATA%\roamies so that installing again brings the same pet back; delete that folder to remove it. The Microsoft Store's copy takes its save with it.

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
