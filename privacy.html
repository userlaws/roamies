The fine print, in plain words.

Roamies is a pet that lives on your desktop. Most of what it knows stays on your computer. This page says what doesn't, where it goes, and how to have it removed.

stays on your computer

Never sent anywhere

  • Your save. Coins, wardrobe, streaks, quests, friends list and settings are one file on your computer.
  • Your open windows. The pet reads their titles and positions so it can walk on them, notice a video site in front, and keep out of the way of a game. It reads the title bar, not what is inside the window.
  • What is playing. The name of the song, so the pet can nod along. Settings > React to music turns that off.

The app has no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

the friend server

What friends need, and no more

While Roamies runs it is connected to the Roamies friend server, so friends can see who is around and pets can visit. The server keeps:

  • your friend code, which your copy makes up by itself. It is not your name.
  • your pet's name and what it is wearing.
  • which version of Roamies you run, and when your copy first and last connected.
  • while you are connected, the friend codes on your friends list.
  • friend requests, accepts and unfriends on their way to someone who is offline, for up to 30 days.
  • gifts Roamies has sent your copy, and whether they arrived.
  • a ban and its reason, if a code or an account has ever been shut out.

Window titles, song names and anything else about your screen never reach it. Coins and ordinary items aren't there either: those live in your save.

The server's own code does not store your IP address. It runs at Neon, in the United States, and the host handles each connection the way any host does.

signing in with Discord

Only if you choose to

Signing in is optional. It lets friends add you by your Discord name instead of your code.

  • Roamies asks Discord one thing: who you are. It receives your Discord id, username, display name and avatar. It does not get your email, your servers or your messages.
  • The friend server keeps those next to your friend code, and a sign-in token that is stored only in scrambled form. Discord's own token is used once and thrown away.
  • Your Discord name shows on your pet card and next to your pet in your friends' lists.
  • Signing out in Roamies removes the link between your Discord account and your friend code.
feedback

Only when you press Send

The speech bubble in the app is for telling us about a bug, an idea, or something you like. Nothing goes anywhere until you press Send. Then the friend server keeps:

  • what you wrote, and which kind of note you said it was.
  • your friend code and your pet's name, and your Discord account if you are signed in, so a note can be told from the next one and answered.
  • a few facts about your copy, so a bug can be tracked down: the version of Roamies and where it was installed from, your system (like Windows 11), how many screens you have and their scaling, whether the friend server was connected, how many friends are on your list, which pet you have and its size and mischief setting, and which tab was open.

Window titles, song names and anything else about your screen are not part of it. We read the notes, and may write a short reply that shows up next to yours in the app. Notes are kept so we can keep track of what was asked for and what was fixed; ask and yours are deleted.

updates, and this website

Other places Roamies talks to

  • Updates. A copy from the installer looks for new versions on GitHub and downloads them from there. A copy from the Microsoft Store is updated by the Store. GitHub and Microsoft see those requests as they see any download.
  • This website. The demo pet's coins and outfit are kept in your own browser and sent nowhere. The page asks GitHub what the newest version is. It sets no cookies and has no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, and its fonts come from Google Fonts.
never

What Roamies does not do

  • It does not sell or hand on anything about you.
  • It does not show ads, in the app or here.
  • It does not read your files, or what is inside your windows.
  • It takes no payment: coins are earned by using the app, and there is nothing to buy with money.
your say

Asking, and having things removed

  • To have what the friend server holds about your friend code or your Discord account removed, or to ask anything about this page, message @roamiesapp on X or open an issue at github.com/userlaws/roamies. Your friend code is on the Friends tab; include it.
  • To remove everything on your computer, uninstall Roamies. The installer's copy leaves your save behind in %APPDATA%\roamies so that installing again brings the same pet back; delete that folder to remove it. The Microsoft Store's copy takes its save with it.
