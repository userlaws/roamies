10:02 AM

Your windows are its furniture.

Roamies draws one click-through layer over your whole desktop. The pet in it knows where your windows are and treats their top edges as ledges.

taskbar

Home is the taskbar

It walks along the top of it. If your taskbar auto-hides, it uses the bottom edge of the screen instead.

ledges

Every title bar is a ledge

It jumps up, wanders along, sits down, sometimes falls asleep there. Maximized windows have no top edge to reach, so it leaves those alone.

drag me

It rides

Drag a window with the pet on it and the pet comes along. Close the window and it drops to whatever is underneath.

Every window on this page drags by its title bar. Double-click one to put it back.

monitors

All your monitors

It walks between side-by-side screens, jumps up or drops down to stacked ones, and poofs across gaps it can't walk.

games

It gets out of the way

Over a fullscreen game it moves to another monitor, or hides until the game closes. Fullscreen video, slides and F11 browsers don't count as games.

8:10 PM

It can leave your screen. It always comes back.

Add a friend and your pets can walk onto each other's desktops. Yours strolls off the edge of your screen, turns up on theirs, leaves a gift box and comes home with a postcard.

rules.txt

Safe to hand your code around

  • Nobody is your friend until you accept. Decline once and their later requests are ignored.
  • A pet that leaves always comes back. When the friend sends it home, if either side drops, or after 100 seconds at the latest.
  • Napping or mid-game? A visitor is sent straight home instead of left waiting.
